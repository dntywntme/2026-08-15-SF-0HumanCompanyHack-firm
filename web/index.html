An agent-run company that employs humans.

Every “AI does your X” product dies at the same place: the ten percent the agent does not know. Broker does not hide that boundary — it prices it. When confidence drops below threshold, the company buys human expertise on the open market, marks it up, and sells the result. Humans are not employees here. They are cost of goods.
